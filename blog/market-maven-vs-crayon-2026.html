What Crayon Does Well

Continuous monitoring. Crayon tracks competitor websites, pricing pages, job postings, and news mentions automatically. It builds historical timelines so you can see how a competitor's messaging evolved over six months. The Salesforce integration means sales reps get updated battlecards without leaving their CRM. If your organization has 20+ salespeople competing against the same three vendors repeatedly, Crayon's automation pays for itself.

What Crayon Doesn't Do

On-demand analysis for new competitors. Quick industry scans when you're exploring an unfamiliar market. The kind of ad-hoc competitive research that a founder or consultant needs at 10 PM before a meeting the next morning. Crayon requires setup — you configure which competitors to track, which pages to monitor, which signals to surface. That setup takes days and assumes you already know who your competitors are.

What Market Maven Does Differently

You describe your market, product, or competitive question in plain language. The AI returns a structured competitive analysis — key players, positioning differences, pricing comparison, and strategic gaps — in minutes. No setup. No configuration. No onboarding call. You ask a question, you get intelligence.

The trade-off is real. Crayon's continuous monitoring catches changes you'd miss with periodic analysis. Market Maven's on-demand model gives you depth on any question instantly but doesn't watch for you while you sleep. They're different tools for different problems.
